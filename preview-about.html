I’m Tanmay Chavan, a photographer, videographer and video editor based in Pune, Maharashtra with roots in Nagpur. I serve as the Multimedia Head at SESA (Software Engineering Student Association) and a Volunteer at V-Click Production (our college multimedia club).

I shoot on my Samsung Galaxy S23 and edit across Adobe Premiere Pro and Lightroom. Whether it's high-octane car meets, bike culture, campus aftermovies, live concerts, or quiet street geometry, I focus on authentic visual rhythm and cinematic atmosphere.
