WHAT
I DO.

I shoot on a Samsung Galaxy S23. Photography, event coverage, reels, video editing — that's what I do. I'm drawn to candid moments, speed, light, and the quiet details most people walk past. I work in Pune, travel when I can, and I'm still building my visual language.

PHILOSOPHY & PROFILE // 04

THE HUMAN
BETWEEN TAKES.

I’m Tanmay Chavan, a 19-year-old student based in Pune with roots in Nagpur. I don’t always go looking for a photograph—I just keep my eyes open for a frame that makes me stop: streets, bikes, cars, skies, and quiet daily moments.

Behind the camera, I slow down and pay attention to light, timing, and small details. I shoot on my Galaxy S23 and edit across Premiere Pro, Lightroom, and CapCut, building my visual language one frame at a time.
