PHILOSOPHY & PROFILE // 01

THE HUMAN
BETWEEN TAKES.

I’m Tanmay Chavan, a photographer, videographer and video editor based in Pune, Maharashtra with roots in Nagpur. I serve as the Multimedia Head at SESA (Software Engineering Student Association) and a Volunteer at V-Click Production (our college multimedia club).

I shoot on my Samsung Galaxy S23 and edit across Adobe Premiere Pro and Lightroom. Whether it's high-octane car meets, bike culture, campus aftermovies, live concerts, or quiet street geometry, I focus on authentic visual rhythm and cinematic atmosphere.

Shot on Galaxy S23. Cut in Premiere Pro. Graded in Lightroom.

DISCIPLINES // 03

WHAT
I DO.

I shoot on a Samsung Galaxy S23. Photography, event coverage, reels, video editing — that's what I do. I'm drawn to candid moments, speed, light, and the quiet details most people walk past. I work in Pune, travel when I can, and I'm still building my visual language.

Photography

Street, candid portraits, automotive culture, architecture, and finding unusual frames in everyday moments.

02

Event Coverage

Capturing the live energy of college fests, concerts, and campus gatherings with dynamic ground coverage.

04
